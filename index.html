Welcome to JUICE BAY

A little juice shop with a big fruity personality.

Tucked into Sai Ratna Arcade on Santosh Nagar Main Road, JUICE BAY is where the neighbourhood stops for something cold, bright and freshly blended.

From watermelon and mosambi to thick chocolate shakes, cool mocktails, fruit bowls and Maggi — the menu is built for hot Hyderabad afternoons, study breaks and evening catch-ups.
